Reflections

What I've learned

October 2026

What I learned from visiting campus offices

Visiting the Clinic and the Registrar's Office showed me how much quiet work goes into keeping a university running. I was surprised by how organized each office is, with clear steps for students and staff who handle many requests a day. It made me realize that good systems, whether in an office or in software, are built so people don't have to figure things out alone.

October 2026

Surviving my first finals

For my Trigonometry and College Algebra finals, I broke my review into short study blocks by lesson and used practice quizzes and flashcards to test myself instead of just rereading notes. Active recall worked far better than passive review. Next quarter I'd start earlier and spread the practice out so I'm not squeezing it into a short window.

October 2026

Building this website

I used AI tools to help with the layout and code, and I learned a lot just by asking why things worked the way they did. To make it my own, I chose the Akatsuki theme, wrote my own content, and tweaked the details like the drifting clouds and the dawn that rises as you scroll. It showed me that AI is most useful as a partner when you stay in charge of the ideas.
